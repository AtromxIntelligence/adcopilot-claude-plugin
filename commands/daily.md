name: daily
description: The daily check-in — what the account spent since yesterday, the searches it showed for, and what changed — as far as the connector's read cap allows.
---

No skill: the connector's tools explain themselves. Three reads, in this order,
stopping at the read cap the server's answer sets and saying which you did not
reach and why: what each campaign spent since yesterday against its daily
budget; the searches it showed for, the wrong ones becoming never-show-for
words (Google's word: negative keywords); what changed in the account and who
changed it — `search` on `change_event`, which needs a `limit`.

The one question, when there is one: whether to block the wrong searches.
